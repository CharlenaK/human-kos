# Upstream Public-Domain & Special Attribution Notices

Three permissive sources included in `human_kos_core.sqlite` are governed by institutional public-domain or attribution terms rather than a standard SPDX template:

## 1. UN ICATUS 2016 (`ICATUS`)
- **Publisher**: United Nations Statistics Division (UNSD)
- **Classification**: International Classification of Activities for Time-Use Statistics (ICATUS 2016)
- **Terms**: Public international statistical standard. Retain attribution to the United Nations Statistics Division when redistributing derived taxonomies.
- **Source**: <https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/time-use/icatus-2016/>
